ANNOUNCEMENT

Thanks to your support, TAMSIC is being renewed.

Thank you, always, for listening to TAMSIC. In Japan we are still a small circle, but from around the world thousands of listeners have found our music. Every one of those voices is what pushes TAMSIC toward the next stage.

01Full-length releases, on YouTube

Songs we had only shared as short samples will now be released in full. Our hope is simple: that the music of no-no, kiki, gEN and TAMSIC reaches one more listener, and finds a quiet place in someone's day.

02The site becomes a platform for artists

The site's paid features (coin-unlocked full listening) will be retired. From here on, TAMSIC will become a place where more artists can share their music with the world. We want to collaborate with artists from many corners of the world — not only Japan. Beyond every border, there are voices that have not yet been heard.

03Later: fan club, goods, and a cycle of support

The music itself will always be free to listen to. As TAMSIC grows, we will gradually build a fan club — with goods and limited content for those who want to walk alongside us.

A portion of the revenue will go to medical support in rural areas and underserved regions overseas. The act of listening to music will slowly — quietly — connect to someone, somewhere, who needs care tomorrow. This is the cycle TAMSIC is aiming for.

Within the TAMJ vision map

TAMSIC is the quiet, underlying current within the vision map drawn by TAmJump. Music connects to local communities, communities to healthcare, healthcare to society. TAMSIC is a small but essential part of that single flowing circle.

See the TAMJ vision map →

04About the coins you have purchased

The coins you purchased will not be lost. They will carry over as credit toward future goods — the exact conversion rate will be announced separately, but we will honor them. There is no expiration date. Please take your time.

Thank you.

We look forward to walking this next stage with you.
